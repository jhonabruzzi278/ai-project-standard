# Architecture

- Start from requirements, observed architecture and constraints; avoid technology replacement without a concrete reason.
- Describe boundaries, responsibilities, contracts, persistence, integrations and failure modes.
- Evaluate security, reliability, performance, operability, maintainability and cost proportionally to risk.
- Record consequential choices as ADR-style decisions with alternatives and tradeoffs.
- Decompose construction into independently reviewable units with explicit dependencies and definitions of done.
- Flag decisions that need product, compliance or operations approval.
