# Delivery

- Infer practices from repository evidence, then ask before turning them into standing rules.
- Build a dependency-aware plan with small units, acceptance evidence and rollback considerations.
- Separate business priority from structural repository health.
- Mark external dependencies, risky migrations, credentials, environment work and owner decisions.
- Keep approval gates at phase boundaries and before any mutation or external action.
- Maintain an audit trail of decisions, skipped stages and reasons.
