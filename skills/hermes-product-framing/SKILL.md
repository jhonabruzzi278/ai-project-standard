# Product Framing

Turn an initial request into a reviewable initiative brief.

- Separate the stated problem from a proposed solution.
- Identify target users, desired outcomes, measurable success and explicit non-goals.
- Mark assumptions and questions instead of inventing business context.
- Reuse observed project evidence when working with an existing product.
- Keep scope adaptive: bug fixes and maintenance should not receive startup-style market analysis unless requested.
- Require human confirmation before treating retained assumptions as requirements.
